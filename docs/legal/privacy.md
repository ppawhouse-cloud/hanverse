## 2. What data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide to us
- **Account data:** the email address you use to register, and a display name you choose.
- **Authentication data:** your password. We never store your password in plain text. It is only kept as a cryptographic hash (a one-way, non-reversible digest) and cannot be read back by us or by anyone else.
- **Support communications:** the content of emails, messages, or tickets you send to our support team.

### 2.2 Data stored locally on your device
- **Learning progress, lesson completions, streaks, settings, and preferences** are stored in your browser's `localStorage`. This data stays on your own device. We do not upload, read, or sync it to our servers, and it is not visible to other users. If you clear your browser data, switch browsers, or use a different device, that local progress may be lost.

### 2.3 Data collected automatically
- **Technical and usage logs:** basic server logs such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, pages visited, timestamps, referring/exit pages, and crash/error reports. We use this to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.
- **Payment confirmation data:** when you subscribe, the payment processor sends us a confirmation that a payment succeeded, the plan you purchased, and a transaction/reference identifier. We do **not** receive or store your full credit card number, bank account number, or full WeChat/PayPal payment credentials.

## 4. Cookies and local storage

- **`localStorage`:** we use your browser's `localStorage` to keep you signed in and to store your learning progress and preferences on your device (see Section 2.2).
- **Essential cookies / tokens:** we may use a small number of strictly necessary cookies or equivalent tokens to maintain your logged-in session and security. Without these, sign-in and subscription features cannot work.
- We do not currently use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If this changes, we will update this Policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

You can clear cookies and site data through your browser settings; note that doing so may sign you out and remove locally stored progress.
