# Refund & After-sales Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10**

This Refund & After-sales Policy explains when and how you can get a refund on HanVerse subscriptions, how cancellations affect your access, and how to report billing issues such as duplicate or unrecognised charges. It applies to digital subscriptions sold through https://www.hanverse.app via PayPal and WeChat Pay.

For general terms, see our **Terms of Service**. For how we handle your data, see our **Privacy Policy**.

---

## 1. Nature of the product

HanVerse is a **digital subscription service**. Once you subscribe, you immediately access online lessons and the AI Tutor. Digital content is delivered electronically and begins to be consumed immediately upon purchase, which affects your refund rights (see below).

---

## 2. Refund window for new subscriptions

You may request a full refund for a new subscription purchase if:

- you contact us within **14 days** of the original purchase date; **and**
- you have **not substantially used** the premium features (for example, you have not completed a significant number of lessons or run the AI Tutor more than a minimal amount).

If both conditions are met, we will issue a refund. If the window has passed, or if you have made substantial use of the Service, refunds are generally **not** provided, except where mandatory consumer law in your country requires otherwise.

**Renewals:** auto-renewal charges are processed at the start of a new billing period. Because you can cancel auto-renewal at any time before renewal (see Section 4), renewal payments are generally **not refundable** after the renewal date. If you believe a renewal was charged without your authorisation, see Section 6 (billing issues).

---

## 3. How refunds are processed, by payment method

Refunds are sent back through the same channel you used to pay. We cannot refund to a different card, account, or payment method.

### 3.1 PayPal
- Refunds are issued back to your PayPal account.
- Typical arrival: **within 3–5 business days** after PayPal processes the refund; in some cases up to 30 days depending on your bank/card linked to PayPal.
- You will receive a confirmation email from PayPal once the refund is completed.

### 3.2 WeChat Pay (微信支付)
- Refunds are issued back to the original WeChat Pay transaction.
- Typical arrival: **within 1–3 business days**, and up to **7–15 business days** for certain bank cards, depending on your issuing bank.
- You can track the refund status in your WeChat Pay transaction history.

We will mark the refund as initiated on our side once the payment provider has processed it; the actual credit to your account depends on PayPal's or WeChat Pay's processing times.

---

## 4. Cancelling auto-renewal and when access ends

- Cancelling **stops future renewals** — it does not refund the current period.
- After you cancel, you keep full access to premium features until the **end of the current paid period** (the date shown in your account or on your payment receipt).
- At the end of that period, your subscription simply does not renew and your access reverts to the free tier.
- How to cancel:
  - **PayPal:** log in to PayPal → Settings / Pre-approved payments → find HanVerse → Cancel.
  - **WeChat Pay:** WeChat → Me → Services → Wallet → Payments & subscriptions / Automatic payments → find HanVerse → cancel.
  - You can also cancel from your HanVerse account settings, where available.

---

## 5. Redemption codes (purchased via mini-shop / 小店)

If you bought a redemption code through our WeChat mini-shop or another authorised channel:

- Unused codes may be returned for a refund within **7 days** of purchase, provided the code has **not been redeemed**.
- Once a code has been redeemed and linked to an account, it is treated as a digital subscription activation and falls under Section 2 (the refund window starts from redemption, not from the mini-shop purchase).
- Lost, stolen, or already-redeemed codes cannot be reissued or refunded unless the fault is on our side.

---

## 6. Duplicate charges, failed payments, or service not received

If you see a charge you do not recognise, were charged twice, or paid but did not receive your subscription:

1. Check your email (including spam) for a receipt from PayPal or WeChat Pay.
2. Check your HanVerse account to see whether the subscription is active.
3. Email us at **ppawhouse@gmail.com** within **30 days** of the charge, and include:
   - the payment date and amount;
   - the payment method (PayPal / WeChat Pay);
   - the transaction reference / order number from your receipt;
   - the email address you used to register.

We will investigate and, if the charge is confirmed as a duplicate or error on our side, we will either extend your subscription or issue a refund through the original payment method.

## 7. Complaint channel and response times

- **Support email:** **ppawhouse@gmail.com** — this is the primary channel for refunds, billing questions, and complaints.
- **First response target:** within **2 business days**.
- **Resolution target:** refund decisions and billing investigations within **10 business days** of receiving all required information. Complex cases (chargebacks, bank delays) may take longer because they depend on PayPal or WeChat Pay.

If we have not resolved your complaint within the expected time, you may also exercise rights through your payment provider (PayPal Resolution Centre, or WeChat Pay support) or your local consumer-protection authority.
