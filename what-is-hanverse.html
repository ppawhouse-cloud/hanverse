What is HanVerse? Learn real Chinese for daily life in China (2026 guide)

最后更新 2026-10-03 · 作者 Allan · HanVerse

HanVerse is a free browser app that teaches spoken Mandarin through 100 real-life scenes across 10 categories, with Chinese 101, 18 culture topics, 100 city guides and a built-in AI tutor.

Why does HanVerse exist?

Most phrasebook apps teach isolated words. HanVerse starts from the situation a newcomer to China actually meets, so the language is attached to a moment you can recognise.

At runtime the site bundles 100 real scenes in 10 categories, plus 100 city guides and 18 culture topics.

How does HanVerse work?

Each scene is a short bilingual dialogue with three choices, so you speak before you memorise rules. Chinese 101 covers tones first, then the AI tutor continues the conversation in the page.

Independent research on generative engines reports that adding statistics, quotations and cited sources raises content visibility by about 30%–40% (Princeton, arXiv:2311.09735).

What do the standards say?

The llms.txt project describes itself as: “The /llms.txt file, helping language models use your website” — AnswerDotAI/llms-txt, see llmstxt.org.

HanVerse therefore ships robots.txt that allows retrieval bots (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, Bytespider, Baiduspider), plus llms.txt, sitemap.xml and FAQ/Article JSON-LD.

FAQ / 常见问题

Is HanVerse free to use?

Yes. It runs in the browser with no paid plan; open the live site and start a scene.

Do I need to install anything?

No install and no account are required; it works on phones and desktops, and the AI tutor chats inside the page.

Which real-life situations are covered?

100 scenes across 10 categories: arrival, food, transport, shopping, living, health, social, errands, leisure and work.

Is it suitable for travel beginners with no HSK level?

Yes. Chinese 101 teaches tones first and every scene gives a short bilingual dialogue, so beginners can speak on day one.
